## Findings

No design-relevant finding for this scope reduction. Binding Developer precisions:

1. The spec's shorthand that the target rejects “symlinked” roots must be read with its explicit upstream-parity instruction, not as a blanket ban on a selected path containing a symlink. Upstream selection resolves the path, and `roomFolder` first resolves stored `realpath`, falling back to `path`, before comparing its hash and judging the canonical folder. Preserve the pinned eligibility and refusal behavior, including its link-walk rules for requested entries; test the actual upstream behavior rather than introducing a stricter root selection rule. A canonical path change fails the hash check; a same-canonical-path replacement need not be detected across operations.
2. Preserve operation-local identity checks, RoomBox generation checks before parser and return, and open's immediate pre-dispatch revalidation. The superseded grant identity review's strict persistent-record, platform qualification, reselection and lost-response requirements do not carry forward; its unrelated R3 and RoomBox protections do.
3. Public tests must exercise a normal selection and an older valid path-only grant through the real producer and guarded consumer, upstream refusal/recovery, and the retained real browser-to-box acceptance. The target normalizer reconstructs known grant fields; do not invent strict unknown-field rejection or silently weaken unrelated existing assertions.

No product code, tests, suites, gates, external service calls or exploit reproductions were run. This review establishes specification fit, not implementation correctness or platform feasibility.
