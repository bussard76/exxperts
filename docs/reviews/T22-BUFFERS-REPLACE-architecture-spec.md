## Alternatives and findings

The prerelease is an unproven but narrowly scoped hypothesis, not proof that the dependency chain or behavior is fixed. Stable 4.4.0 with `unzipper@0.11.6` overridden falls outside its declared `^0.10.11` and is unsupported by that declaration; it needs a separately reviewed and explicitly approved trial plus equivalent graph, compatibility and safety evidence. SheetJS 0.20.3 already exists, but replacing this renderer changes the API and risks styles, themes, merges, geometry and formula fidelity; it is a distinct proposal requiring an exact equivalence spec and review, not a fallback automatically authorized here. Removing XLSX preview conflicts with the approved product scope and requires Sascha's product decision. If all bounded candidates fail, report the evidence to Front rather than silently choosing a weaker behavior.

Registry metadata and streamed source inspection are read-only and do not establish integrity, licence sufficiency, Node compatibility, fidelity or shipped contents.
